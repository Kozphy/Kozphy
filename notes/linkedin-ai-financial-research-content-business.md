# 11. Don't Fake Credentials

Important distinction:

```text
Learn presentation style
        ✅

Copy someone's credentials
        ❌
```

I can learn from strong LinkedIn profiles:

- structure
- positioning
- visual identity
- writing style
- portfolio presentation

But every claim about:

- employment
- internship
- university
- certification
- research
- investment performance

must be accurate and verifiable.
